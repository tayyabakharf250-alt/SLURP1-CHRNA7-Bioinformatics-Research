# SLURP1 and CHRNA7 Bioinformatics Research

## Project Overview
This project focuses on the bioinformatics analysis of SLURP1, a gene associated with Mal de Meleda (MDM), and selected SLURP1 mutations. It also includes exploratory structural and docking analysis involving CHRNA7.

## Research Objectives
- Collect and organize reported SLURP1 mutation data.
- Analyze the wild-type (WT) SLURP1 protein and selected mutant variants.
- Compare predicted protein structures.
- Explore the possible interaction between SLURP1 and CHRNA7 through molecular docking.

## Variants Studied
- Wild Type (WT)
- G86R
- C77R
- P82S
- R71H
- R71P

## Tools and Resources
- Python
- Biopython
- NCBI
- UniProt
- AlphaFold
- ColabFold
- PyMOL
- Molecular docking tools

## Project Structure
- `mutation_data/` – Mutation sequences, annotations, and verification files.
- `processed_data/` – Processed and integrated research data.
- `structures/` – Protein structures, structural comparisons, and figures.
- `docking/` – CHRNA7 receptor and docking-related files.

## Research Approach
The study uses computational methods to examine sequence information, predicted protein structures, mutation-related structural differences, and exploratory protein docking.

## Important Note
Protein structures generated by prediction tools and docking results are computational models. Docking results are exploratory and do not establish a confirmed biological interaction between SLURP1 and CHRNA7. Experimental validation is required.

## Research Status
Computational research project – ongoing development and analysis.
